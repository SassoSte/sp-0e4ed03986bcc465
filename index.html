AI usage stress case

Worst case: every client a heavy user, a runaway session, a looping bug, and vendor price or model changes. Daniel names no model. Each reply resends the conversation so far.

Clients, all heavyDanielHaiku 4.5Sonnet 5.5Haiku 5.5
10$18$19 / $44$46 / $115$2 / $6
25$45$48 / $110$114 / $287$6 / $14
100$180$190 / $441$457 / $1,147$25 / $57
Runaway, 1 clientn/a$4 / $37$7 / $97$2 / $21

Monthly AI cost, cached / uncached. The Daniel column is his rate: 450 replies × $0.004 per client. The other columns are 720 replies per client. The runaway row is one 7-hour session, not a month.

Assumptions

  • 2 sessions a day, 12 turns each, 30 days: 720 replies per client a month, 1.6 × his heavy case of 450.
  • Each turn sends a 2,000-token system prompt, the history so far and a 150-token message. Each reply is 300 tokens, thinking included.
  • Each session starts cold; later turns read the system prompt and history from Anthropic's 5-minute prompt cache.
  • Runaway: one client, one turn a minute for 7 hours (400 turns), context reaching about 182,000 tokens (236,000 on the newer tokenizer).
  • Prices per million input / output tokens, Anthropic pricing page, Oct 16, 2026: Haiku 4.5 $1 / $5 (cache write $1.25, read $0.10); Sonnet 5.5 $2 / $10 (write $2.50, read $0.10); Haiku 5.5 $0.10 / $0.50, or $0.50 / $2.50 for the whole request once a prompt passes 100,000 tokens.
  • Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 use Anthropic's newer tokenizer, about 30% more tokens for the same text, so their token counts are × 1.3.
  • Storage, hosting and email are outside the AI columns: Supabase $25 a month ($30 at 1,000 clients), Vercel $20, Resend $20 from about 100 clients.

Looping bug

A bug resending a 200,000-token conversation every 10 seconds, uncached Haiku 4.5: 360 calls an hour × 200,000 tokens at $1 per million = $72 an hour of input, $72.54 with output. $500 / $72.54 = 6.9 hours to the Start-tier cap. Rate limits do not stop it: 1.2M input tokens a minute against the Start tier's 2M. At the cap the API stops and the companion is offline for every client until the 1st of the next month.

Price and model changes

Anthropic announced a Sonnet 5 increase to $3 / $15 for Sept 1, 2026, then withdrew it. Models retire with at least 60 days' notice; Sonnet 4.5 was deprecated Sept 30 and retires Nov 30, 2026. Under the current offer a price rise or a forced migration falls to Solunara.
